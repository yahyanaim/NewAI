## Transformation Complete

Successfully transformed the existing geopolitical application into a sophisticated WarTracker24-style intelligence platform.

### Major Features Implemented

1. **Flash Updates Header** - Scrolling ticker with LIVE indicator
2. **Three-Column Layout** - 30% (Live Signals) | 40% (3D Globe) | 30% (AI Assessment)
3. **Live Signals Feed** - Event cards with sentiment/priority filtering
4. **Enhanced 3D Globe** - Color-coded animated event markers (green/red/orange)
5. **AI Assessment Panel** - 5 scoring dimensions with progress bars
6. **Dark Intelligence Theme** - Teal/cyan accents on deep blue background
